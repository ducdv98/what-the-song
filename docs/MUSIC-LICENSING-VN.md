# Vietnamese music licensing research

Checked 30 September 2026 for this game's current clip workflow. This is a source-backed research note, not a determination that any particular song is licensed.

## Current primary source

The National Gazette's [consolidated Intellectual Property Law, 67/VBHN-VPQH (23 March 2026)](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/van-ban-hop-nhat-so-67-vbhn-vpqh-469197.htm) incorporates the amendments through [Law 131/2025/QH15](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/luat-so-131-2025-qh15-468691/61687.htm), effective 1 April 2026. The [official consolidated PDF](https://congbao.cdnchinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/4/10/469197-1775632354_v1_1775783230_signed.pdf) gives the operative article text cited below. Article and paragraph references are more durable than PDF page numbers.

## What the law says

- A musical composition is a protected work (Article 14(1)(d)). The author or copyright owner has exclusive rights to copy **all or part** of it and communicate it to the public over electronic networks, including on-demand access (Article 20(1)(c), (đ), (2)). [Source: consolidated law, Articles 14 and 20](https://congbao.cdnchinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/4/10/469197-1775632354_v1_1775783230_signed.pdf#page=18).
- A recorded performance and the sound recording have **separate neighboring rights**. The performance owner controls copying all or part of the fixed performance and communication to the public (Article 29(3)(b), (e), (4)); the recording owner controls copying all or part of the recording and communicating it to the public (Article 30(1)(a), (d), (2)). [Source: consolidated law, Articles 29–30](https://congbao.cdnchinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/4/10/469197-1775632354_v1_1775783230_signed.pdf#page=26).
- Article 25(1) lists specific copyright exceptions, including personal scientific research or study, teaching under its conditions, and quotation to comment on, introduce or illustrate one's own work. Article 25(2) further requires that use not conflict with normal exploitation or unreasonably prejudice the right holder. Merely being short, private, free to play, or noncommercial is **not itself** a listed blanket exception. [Source: consolidated law, Article 25](https://congbao.cdnchinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/4/10/469197-1775632354_v1_1775783230_signed.pdf#page=21).
- Article 32(1) likewise limits neighboring-right exceptions to listed uses such as individual study, direct noncommercial teaching, and current-news quotation, subject to Article 32(2). A song-identification game does not obviously fit those categories. The latter is an **application/inference**, not an explicit ruling about games. [Source: consolidated law, Article 32](https://congbao.cdnchinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/4/10/469197-1775632354_v1_1775783230_signed.pdf#page=29).
- Articles 26 and 33 provide certain uses of published works/recordings without advance permission **but with royalties and conditions**. In particular, Article 33(1) concerns specified commercial uses of published recordings for broadcasting, retransmission, communication to the public, or business activity. It does not supply a general free license for short game clips. [Sources: consolidated law, Article 26](https://congbao.cdnchinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/4/10/469197-1775632354_v1_1775783230_signed.pdf#page=23) and [Article 33](https://congbao.cdnchinhphu.vn/180507251028987904/2026/4/10/469197-1775632354_v1_1775783230_signed.pdf#page=30).

## Application to this repo

`tools/ingest.py` downloads audio with `yt-dlp`, cuts clips, and writes them to `apps/web/public/assets/songs`; the site serves those files to players. On the cited provisions, downloading and storing clips implicates reproduction rights, and serving on-demand clips implicates communication rights. This is an **inference from the statutory rights and the code**, not a court finding. The script's description of the project as “private, non-commercial” does not itself establish an exception. The script also notes that downloading audio conflicts with YouTube's Terms of Service; platform permission and Vietnamese copyright permission are distinct questions.

The operationally sound route is to document permission or another verified legal basis **before** ingesting each track, covering the composition and the particular performance/recording, including reproduction, excerpting, storage and online playback as applicable. Where cover art is used, review its separate image rights too. A site terms page can accurately explain status, ownership, complaints and removal, but **cannot grant a license from third-party rights holders or cure an unlicensed copy**. Attribution and removal on request do not replace prior permission. These are practical conclusions from the cited rights and exceptions, not independent statutory exemptions.

## Cloud storage on Tencent COS

From the COS migration (`docs/adr/0003-clips-in-private-cos-bucket.md`), each published clip and cover is also stored with a third-party host, Tencent Cloud Object Storage in Hong Kong or Singapore, and served to players through short-lived signed URLs. For the reproduction and communication rights above, COS is an additional storage location and an additional hosting provider, so any permission documented for a track should cover storage and playback there as well.

The migration uploads every song already ingested (401 at the time of writing). None of them was individually recorded as licensed before ingest, so the upload widens where existing copies sit rather than introducing a new kind of use, and it does not cure the gap described above. A per-song "cleared" flag that gates upload is a recorded follow-up in the ADR, not part of the migration.
