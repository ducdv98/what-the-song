# Person photos are used without a licence or a Credit

The People Topic uses photos of public figures that are already published on the internet, with no licence check and no **Credit** shown. Food does the opposite (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 only, Credit after the Round), and we chose differently here on purpose: Commons holds almost nothing for streamers and influencers, so a licensed-only rule would leave several fields unplayable.

We accept the cost knowingly. A photo being online does not remove its copyright, and a person's likeness may carry rights of its own in some jurisdictions. A rights-holder or a Person can send a takedown or a claim, and the project has no licence to defend itself with. The exposure grows with the game's audience.

Decisions made to contain the risk:

- Only public figures with an established public profile are Persons; never private individuals or minors.
- A Person is removed, with their photo, on request.
- Each Person's catalogue entry stores the photo's source URL, and ingest requires it. The game never shows it. It exists so a photo can be found and removed quickly.
- Credit stays a Dish-only concept; a Person has none.

Rejected: Commons plus permissioned photos only (safest, but too thin for streamers and influencers); showing a source link after the Round (a good-faith signal, but it does not remove liability and was not wanted on the result screen).

Revisit if a claim arrives, if the game gains a large audience, or if it is monetised.
