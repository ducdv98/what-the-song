## Notes

### Uncertain regions (please decide)

Dishes below carry the region shown in the table, but the owner may want a different one. Where the home region is really unclear the default is Toàn quốc.

- **Region assigned, but could be Toàn quốc or another region:** Bánh xèo (Nam, but also Trung), Bánh chưng (Bắc; eaten nationwide at Tết, with Bánh tét as the Nam/Trung counterpart), Bò kho and Thịt kho tàu (Nam, widely eaten everywhere), Bún chả cá (Trung: Nha Trang and Đà Nẵng versions), Bánh căn (south-central coast and Đà Lạt), Bánh gai (Thanh Hóa, Bắc), Thịt trâu gác bếp (Tây Bắc, filed under Bắc), Bánh bột chiên (Chợ Lớn Hoa community, Nam), Bánh cam (Nam name; Bắc `bánh rán` is a different pastry, not used as an Alias).
- **Tây Nguyên is thin (2 Dishes: Cơm lam, Rượu cần).** Both are shared with the northern highlands (the Rượu cần photo is captioned Mường, Hòa Bình; the Cơm lam photo is captioned Tây Nguyên). The owner may prefer Toàn quốc for Rượu cần. I found no usable CC photo for other Tây Nguyên dishes (Gà nướng cơm lam, Canh thụt, Phở khô Gia Lai, Heo cắp nách).

### Alias collisions

A script folded every name and Alias the way `looseKey` in `packages/core/src/vietnamese.ts` does (strip diacritics, đ to d, lowercase, punctuation to spaces) and compared them across all 80 Dishes: **no collisions**. Near-misses worth knowing about:

- `chả cá` (Alias of Chả cá Lã Vọng) is a substring of Bún chả cá, Chả giò and Giò lụa/chả lụa, but not equal to any of them.
- `bánh dày` (Alias of Bánh giầy) and `bánh rán`/`bánh cam`: `bánh rán` was deliberately left out as an Alias of Bánh cam because it names a different pastry in Bắc.
- `thịt kho` and `cá kho` (Aliases of Thịt kho tàu and Cá kho tộ) are generic and would collide with a future Thịt kho trứng or Cá kho Dish.
- `nem nướng` (Alias of Nem nướng Nha Trang) would collide with a future plain Nem nướng Dish.
- `bún đậu` and `bún bò` style short forms: `bún đậu` is an Alias of Bún đậu mắm tôm; `bún bò` was left off Bún bò Huế so it does not clash with Bún bò Nam Bộ.
- English Aliases kept to commonly used glosses only: `egg coffee`, `summer roll`, `balut`, `white rose`.

### Method and limits

- Candidates were found by Commons title search and by Commons categories (for example `Category:Bánh`, `Category:Bún dishes`). Only files whose Commons licence short name is CC BY or CC BY-SA (any 2.x, 3.0, 4.0 version) or CC0 were considered. Licence, author and file page were re-fetched from the API for the final 80.
- Author values are the Commons `Artist` field with HTML removed; the owner should still check Credit wording before issue 05 (a few are Flickr or Vietnamese-Wikipedia usernames).
- Tier is my estimate of national fame, not photo difficulty. Please correct Tiers, regions and Aliases in this file or in `candidates.jsonl` before issue 05 starts.
